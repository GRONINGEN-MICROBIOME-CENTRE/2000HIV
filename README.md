# 2000HIV Study

The **2000HIV study** is a longitudinal cohort study in which 1,895 virally suppressed people living with HIV were recruited between 2019 and 2021 from four Dutch HIV treatment centers: OLVG in Amsterdam, Erasmus MC in Rotterdam, Elisabeth-TweeSteden Ziekenhuis in Tilburg, Radboudumc in Nijmegen.

For the majority, the cohort comprises White men who have sex with men (MSM). However, the cohort also includes approximately 10% Black participants and 15% female participants. Additionally, several distinct extreme HIV clinical phenotypes were included, which are elite controllers, immunological non-responders and rapid progressors. The 2000HIV cohort therefore represents a heterogeneous study population, allowing for a wide range of comparisons and stratifications within the cohort.

The cohort is described in:

**Vos W, Groenendijk A, et al.** *The 2000HIV study: Design, multi-omics methods and participant characteristics.*  
Frontiers in Immunology – Viral Immunology, 20 December 2022.
**DOI:** [10.3389/fimmu.2022.982746](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2022.982746/full)
